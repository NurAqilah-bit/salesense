What goes in a SalesSense report?

Example structure

Each report can combine key statistics, visual summaries, plain-language findings and areas worth investigating. The examples below use the same simulated Grandsandwich-style dataset as this prototype.

Monthly Sales Summary

Example report preview

Total revenueBND 50,422
Total orders3,605
Avg. orderBND 13.99
Best monthAugust

Example finding: August recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately BND 7,100. A full report could compare this with previous months and highlight changes in order volume and average order value.

Product Performance Report

Example report preview

Highest revenue productAssorted Sandwich PlatterBND 8,873
Lowest revenue productIced Lemon TeaBND 1,173

Example finding: Assorted Sandwich Platter is the strongest product by revenue. The report could help a user compare high- and low-performing products before investigating factors such as demand, pricing or availability.

Business Insight Report

Example report preview

SalesSense highlights Friday is the strongest day 56.5% of demo orders are takeaway

Example interpretation: SalesSense could flag patterns that deserve further investigation, such as why Friday performs strongly or whether takeaway customers purchase a different mix of products.

Example report sections

Suggested contents
1. Executive SummaryShort overview of the most important findings.
2. Key Performance IndicatorsRevenue, orders, average order value and other selected KPIs.
3. Trends & VisualisationsCharts showing monthly, product, day and channel performance.
4. SalesSense InsightsPlain-language explanations and suggested areas for further investigation.
